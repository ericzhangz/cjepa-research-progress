For both initialized and prior bounded-D offsets, all 44 stationary arrivals have bitwise identical prototype means and likelihoods; their observation posterior is bitwise equal to both the issued prior and the supplied public weights, including unequal-prior checks. Native nominal H1–H4 matches F0 exactly. The maximum incoming-public-to-returned difference is 0. No detector, threshold, or static-action update gate was added.
