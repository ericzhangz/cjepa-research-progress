No fitting, node selection, hyperparameter tuning, simulator rollout or FourRoom execution.

The branch means remain [-v,+v]. The original covariance is I+xi*D D^T. The bounded covariance is I+xi*P with P=D(G+I)^-1 D^T, rho=lambda=1 in fixed whitened units. Bounding changes the noise geometry, not the branch means.

The prescribed fixed-xi log Bayes factor is 2v^2/(1+2xi*v^2) for the original geometry. It saturates at 1/xi. For the bounded geometry it is 2v^2/[1+xi*2v^2/(1+2v^2)].
